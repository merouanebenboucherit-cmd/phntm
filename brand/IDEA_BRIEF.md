# PHNTM — brand & idea brief for image generation

## The pitch in one sentence
PHNTM is an open-source, local-first CLI that turns any USB stick into a
*légendary* bootable arsenal — you pick a persona (IT Tech, Pentester, DFIR,
Privacy), it builds the perfect Ventoy stick with live ISOs, encrypted vault,
LUKS persistence, and tools. Zero telemetry. No host. No cloud. Just your
stick and your rules.

## Prompt A — photorealistic hero image (product idea)
> Photorealistic product hero, cinematic dark tech mood. On a matte black
> desk, a single USB flash drive stands upright like a small monolith,
> illuminated by a faint emerald-glow LED ring on its cap. The drive's body
> is subtly translucent smoked glass showing internal layers glowing through:
> a bootloader ring, stacked ISO discs, a padlock icon (encrypted vault), a
> circuit-board trace forming the letters "PHNTM". Behind it, a deep-space
> holographic UI projection floats: four colored persona chips (wrench,
> crosshair/target, magnifying glass, sunglasses) feeding into a vertical
> ladder of four tiers labeled 16 / 32 / 64 / 128, arrows flowing right into
> a stylized boot-menu wheel. Ambient bokeh, volumetric emerald + ice-blue
> rim light, faint matrix-code rain barely visible in the background,
> hyper-detailed 8k, shallow depth of field. No people, no text other than
> the "PHNTM" hologram.

## Prompt B — logo concept art (for reference)
> Minimalist cyber logo concept, flat vector style on near-black (#0B0F14).
> A USB-A connector whose rectangular body is fused with a friendly ghost
> silhouette — rounded head, wavy hem, two hollow emerald eyes (glowing
> #00FF87). Tiny emerald status-LED dot on the ghost's crown. One thin dashed
> orbit ring around the mark suggesting a bootable disc. Wordmark "PHNTM" in
> bold monospace below, small lime subtitle "LEGENDARY USB". Monochrome +
> single neon accent, crisp geometric, badge/avatar-friendly.
